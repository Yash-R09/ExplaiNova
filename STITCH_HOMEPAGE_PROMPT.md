# Explainova — Google Stitch Homepage Prompt

## Scope

Design only the public marketing homepage, not the logged-in dashboard. Create one coherent design direction with responsive desktop and mobile layouts of this same page. Do not generate login, dashboard, editor, pricing, or settings pages. We will iterate on this homepage before approving a shared design language for other screens.

## Copy-ready prompt

Design a premium public homepage for Explainova, an AI-assisted studio that turns educational ideas into original 30–90-second animated explainer videos with narration and synchronized captions.

The audience is adult educational creators, educators, and subject experts. The product workflow is Plan an idea → Review a combined script/storyboard → Generate the video. Creators can export or approve publishing to their own YouTube/Instagram accounts. Do not imply that the app publishes without approval.

Create ONLY the homepage. Give it the art direction of a contemporary independent animation studio combined with a precise creative tool. It should feel confident, vibrant, tactile, and carefully composed. Show the actual product idea through educational animation previews and storyboard fragments.

VISUAL DIRECTION

- Warm ivory #F6F3EB as the dominant background, near-black ink #171923 for primary text.
- Electric cobalt #3157FF as the principal brand accent; tangerine #FF784F and acid-lime #D9F26C used sparingly for emphasis and selected illustration surfaces.
- Color should come from intentional large surfaces and original diagrams, not rainbow decorations. Keep the interface restrained enough that the video examples remain the focus.
- Oversized editorial typography, tight but readable display spacing, generous margins, fine dividers, and a strong grid. Use Sora for headlines and Manrope for body/interface copy, or close available alternatives.
- Desktop content width approximately 1240 px within a 1440 px canvas. Hero headline approximately 80–92 px; body 18–20 px. Mobile heading approximately 44–52 px.
- Small interface surfaces may use 12–16 px radii; featured video frames can use 24 px. Avoid rounding every element into a pill. Shadows should be subtle and purposeful.
- Brand motif: a simple frame/play symbol and a continuous line connecting idea, storyboard, and finished video. No sparkle/AI-wand icon as the central brand device.
- No generic purple-gradient hero, gradient lettering, floating glass cards, dashboard screenshots as filler, robot illustrations, excessive glow, random blobs, or identical feature-card grids.

PAGE COMPOSITION

1. Navigation: Explainova wordmark on the left. Examples and How it works as in-page links. Sign in as a quiet text action and Create a video as the primary button. Keep it light and compact.

2. Hero: asymmetric layout, approximately 55% typography and 45% visual composition.
   Eyebrow: MAKE KNOWLEDGE MOVE.
   Headline: “Big ideas. Clearly explained.”
   Supporting copy: “Turn a learning idea into a short animated video—with a storyboard you can shape, a voice you choose, and captions that follow every word.”
   Primary CTA: “Create a video”. Secondary action: “Watch an example”.
   Under the actions, understated text: “30–90 seconds · Voiceover + captions · Made for vertical video”.
   Right side: a strong 9:16 animated-video preview composition, not a phone-device mockup. Feature a beautifully drawn headphone illustration and opposing sound waves against a deep violet/cyan studio scene. Include a readable example caption inside the video, a discreet play affordance, and a small 0:53 duration marker. Place one restrained storyboard fragment behind it with a connecting line to demonstrate idea-to-video progression; do not scatter unrelated floating UI cards.

3. Examples: headline “A different subject. A different visual world.” Create a distinctive gallery of three vertical educational-video previews with generous spacing. Topics: The science of quiet (violet/cyan waveforms); How rain returns to the sky (ocean blue/sea green water cycle); Why satellites stay in orbit (midnight blue/solar amber orbital diagram). Each thumbnail should communicate its topic through original graphics. Titles and approximate example durations appear outside the preview. The orbit example is illustrative; do not invent a claim that it is an existing finished customer video. No decorative bottom tabs/pills in the previews.

4. Workflow: a wide editorial composition titled “From a thought to a finished explanation.” Present three connected stages, not three identical cards: Plan your idea; Shape the storyboard; Make it move. Show small relevant UI fragments: an idea prompt, two editable scene cards with narration beside visuals, and a finished vertical preview. Embed script review within storyboard review; do not introduce a separate script wizard or complex timeline.

5. Creative control: one larger split feature section headed “Keep your voice. Find your visual style.” Pair a large diagram-led visual with concise feature copy: male/female voice choices with named voice previews; topic-matched illustration styles; editable captions; scene-level corrections. Show controls as product illustration, not an entire dashboard. Use no provider names or API configuration in the user-facing design.

6. Closing CTA: a bold cobalt surface with a minimal original animated-line motif. Headline “What will you explain next?” and Create a video action. Footer with wordmark, Privacy, Terms, Contact, and concise product description. No invented social metrics or copyright ownership claims for external brands.

PRODUCT AND CONTENT RULES

- No pricing, billing, credit counters, paid-plan comparisons, fabricated testimonials, customer logos, awards, or invented user counts.
- No promises of views, monetization, guaranteed accuracy, instant rendering, or unlimited free usage.
- No children, nursery-rhyme characters, avatars, or singing features.
- Keep marketing copy short, specific, and related to explaining ideas. Avoid “revolutionize”, “unlock limitless creativity”, and similar filler.
- Retain captions; omit decorative bottom label pills and segmented progress tabs from video examples. Diagram labels remain allowed when educationally useful.
- Buttons should have obvious hover/focus treatments. Example playback should be an honest preview interaction; do not fake a completed generation or create missing pages.

RESPONSIVE AND INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

- At mobile widths, place copy before the hero preview, collapse navigation cleanly, preserve readable video captions, and use a single-column or intentional horizontal example gallery without page overflow.
- Accessible contrast, visible keyboard focus, labeled playback controls, approximately 44 px minimum touch targets, and support reduced motion in any proposed animation.
- Use restrained motion: waveform movement, line drawing, subtle preview transitions. Do not animate every surface. No audio autoplay.

DELIVERABLE

Create one polished homepage direction with desktop and mobile variants of the SAME page. Preserve the chosen colors, typography, spacing, button styles, radii, and illustration principles as reusable design tokens. Stop after the homepage. We will refine it before applying this design language to the rest of Explainova.

## Iteration rule

Edit the existing homepage in the same Stitch project rather than generating unrelated projects for each adjustment. Freeze an approved screen/version and its tokens only after founder review. Use that exact screen as reference when explicitly asked to design additional pages; do not generate them in advance.
